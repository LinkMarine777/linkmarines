HOW THE SCORE WORKS

Every launch of the last 24 h that trades ($2,500+ volume, or graduated) gets a score out of 100:

  • Organic buyers (30): distinct wallets Jupiter counts as organic buyers in 24 h. Bots and wash wallets are filtered out.
  • Fee-backed volume (20): what the coin actually paid its holders, divided by its tax rate, against the volume it reports. Every taxed trade pays, so real trading comes out near 1.0. Volume washed through untaxed routes comes out far below it: farms measure 0.15–0.45.
  • Holders (15) and top holders' share (10), from Jupiter.
  • Dev record (15): the deployer's other launches over the last 14 days. 1 in 5 graduated, or one peaked at $500k+, gives the full 15. A serial launcher (5+ coins, under 1 in 10 graduated) gets 0.
  • Organic share of volume (10), from Jupiter.

REAL 60+ · WATCH 35+ · FARM $20,000+ volume that the fees (or, with no payouts to check, the organic buyers) don't back up · THIN the rest.

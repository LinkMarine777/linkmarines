#WalletHolds≈ Value7d
The wallets that together own 80% of the supply (pools, lockers and other program accounts excluded); 7d = balance change (CLOBr). Rewards: each wallet's share of every payout, counted from its first one, so the buy that got it in doesn't count. Score = 60% how much of their rewards they bought back in + 25% how often (payout days they bought more than they sold and didn't sell it back within 7 days) + 15% buying beyond their rewards (8× or more counts in full); net sellers score 0; no wallet counts for more than 15%; under 10 wallets or $2K of rewards shows as low data. It shows whether the biggest holders reinvest their rewards: a gauge of holder behaviour, not a price prediction. One label per wallet, from what it did with this coin: SELLER (net sold since its first reward), else TRADER (sold back half or more of what it bought within 7 days), else COMPOUNDER (put back 50%+ of its rewards), PARTIAL (10-50%) or COLLECTOR (under 10%). Buys and sells are balance changes, so moving tokens between your own wallets shows as a trade. Refreshed every 6 h.

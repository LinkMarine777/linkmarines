1

Hold $20+ of $MARINE

That's the only requirement. Any wallet holding at least $20 of $MARINE is in. Nothing to register, stake or lock.

2

Every trade pays a 3% tax

A 3% tax comes out of every buy and every sell of $MARINE. On top of that, the pool's trading fees are taken in $LINK. More trading means a faster-filling pot.

3

The tax is swapped into $LINK

Stonkfun, the platform $MARINE launched on, swaps the collected tax into $LINK and adds it to the pot. You can watch it fill live on the stream.

4

At $250, it's airdropped

When the pot reaches $250, stonkfun sends it out as $LINK straight to every eligible holder's wallet. We call it a pulse. Then the pot starts filling again, and the wheel keeps turning.

Payouts land on their own. If you don't see $LINK in your wallet app, add the $LINK token to your token list.

Questions

Do I need to claim anything?

No. $LINK is sent to your wallet automatically. There's nothing to click or sign.

How often are payouts?

Whenever the pot hits $250. Busy trading days fill it faster, so payouts come more often. Check the countdown on the stream.

Does the 3% apply when I buy and when I sell?

Yes, both. That tax is what pays holders. If a swap fails, raise slippage a little.

Where do I watch it happen?

On The War Room: every payout, buy and whale move shows up live, 24/7.

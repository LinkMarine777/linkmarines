STEP 2

Bridge to Solana with Jumper

Jumper moves your crypto from another chain (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and more) to Solana.

  • Go to jumper.xyz and connect the wallet that holds your funds.
  • Pick the chain and token you're sending from.
  • Pick Solana as the destination and SOL as the token, then paste your new Solana wallet address.
  • Confirm and wait. Most bridges land in a few minutes.
Keep a little SOL for fees (about $2 is plenty). Every Solana transaction needs a tiny bit of SOL, even when you pay with something else.
